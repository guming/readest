The Design of Everyday Things

Knowledge in the Head and in the World

Human memory is not a perfect storage cabinet. People rely on signs, constraints, mappings, and feedback in the world to complete routine actions. Good design shifts cognitive load from recall to recognitionA1.

A useful interface exposes enough structure that the next action feels legible. The user should not need to memorize an invisible model before acting.

When mappings are natural and feedback is immediate, users can recover from mistakes and build reliable habits through interaction. A2
